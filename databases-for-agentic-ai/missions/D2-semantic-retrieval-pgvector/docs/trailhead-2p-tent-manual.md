# Trailhead 2P Tent: Owner's Manual

The Trailhead 2P is a freestanding, double-wall backpacking tent for two people. It has a packed weight of 1.9 kg including the inner tent, rainfly, one pre-bent aluminium hub pole, 8 stakes and 4 guylines. The floor measures 215 by 130 centimetres, with a peak height of 105 centimetres, and there are two doors and two vestibules. The rainfly and floor are silicone-coated nylon with a hydrostatic head of 3000 millimetres on the fly and 5000 millimetres on the floor.

## First pitch

Practise the first pitch at home. Lay the inner tent flat with the doors where you want them. Assemble the hub pole by letting the shock cord pull each section together; never flick the pole sections into place, because the impact cracks the ferrules. Clip the four pole ends into the grommets at the corners of the inner tent, then attach the inner tent's plastic clips to the pole, starting from the hub. Throw the rainfly over the frame with the colour-coded corner straps matching the coloured webbing on the inner tent, and buckle the four corners. Tension the corner straps evenly until the fly is taut and does not touch the inner tent anywhere.

## Staking and guying in wind

Stake all four corners at 45 degrees, leaning away from the tent, and stake out both vestibules. In winds above 30 kilometres per hour, also attach the four guylines to the guy-out loops halfway up the fly and stake them at least one metre from the tent. Pitch with the narrow foot end facing into the wind. Re-tension the fly after dark, because nylon stretches when it is cold and damp.

## Condensation

Condensation inside the fly is normal in cool, humid or still conditions; it is not a leak. Open both upper vents and at least one door's top zip, pitch away from rivers and lake shores, and avoid cooking inside the vestibule. Wipe the inside of the fly with a small towel in the morning.

## Care

Never store the tent wet: dry it completely within 24 hours to prevent mildew, which permanently damages the coatings and is not covered by the warranty. Clean with lukewarm water and a sponge only; do not machine wash. Keep the tent out of strong sun for long periods, because UV light weakens nylon. Broken pole sections can be replaced: Riverbend stores stock spare sections and the 2P pole repair sleeve is in the stuff sack. Tents carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects.

# INTERNAL - Staff discount and price-override matrix

For store staff only. Never share with customers.

Store managers may override prices by up to 25 percent to match a competitor. Team members get 40 percent off Riverbend-branded gear and 20 percent off partner brands with code RB-STAFF40. Damaged-box items can be sold at 30 percent off without manager approval. Price overrides above 25 percent require the regional manager's approval in the point-of-sale system, and every override is reviewed weekly.

# Ultralight Stove: Safety Instructions and Operating Guide

The Riverbend Ultralight Stove is a canister-top stove that burns isobutane-propane gas from screw-thread (EN 417, Lindal valve) canisters. It weighs 82 grams, produces 2.6 kilowatts and boils one litre of water in about four minutes at sea level with no wind. Read these instructions completely before first use and keep them with the stove.

## Critical safety warnings

Use the stove outdoors only. Never use it inside a tent, vestibule, car, cabin or any enclosed space: burning gas produces carbon monoxide, an odourless gas that can kill, and the flame can ignite tent fabric in seconds. Keep the stove at least 1.5 metres from flammable material. Never leave a lit stove unattended. Do not use pots wider than 22 centimetres, because oversized pots reflect heat back onto the canister and can overheat it. Never use a windscreen that surrounds both the stove and the canister, for the same reason.

## Connecting and checking for leaks

Check that the canister's thread and the stove's rubber O-ring are clean and undamaged. Close the flame-control valve fully by turning it clockwise. Screw the stove onto the canister by hand until it is snug; do not over-tighten. Listen and smell for leaking gas. If you hear hissing or smell gas, unscrew the stove immediately, away from any flame, and check the O-ring. A stove that leaks gas at the connection must not be used. A damaged O-ring can be replaced with the spare supplied in the case.

## Lighting and use

Place the canister on firm, level ground, unfold the three pot supports until they click, open the valve a quarter turn and light the gas at the side of the burner with a match or lighter. Adjust the flame so it does not extend beyond the bottom of the pot. In cold weather below 0 degrees Celsius, keep the canister warm in your jacket before use, because gas pressure drops as temperature falls. After cooking, close the valve, let the stove cool for at least five minutes before touching the burner, and unscrew it from the canister.

## Returns and service

For safety reasons, stoves that have been used can only be returned within 30 days, and only if they are defective. Unused stoves in original packaging follow the standard 60-day return policy. Never attempt to repair the valve yourself.

# Riverbend Outfitters Returns Policy

We want you to love your gear. If something is not right, you can return most items within 60 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method, in-store or by post, as long as the item is unused, clean and in its original condition with tags attached. Items bought online can be returned to any Riverbend store, or by post using a prepaid return label that Trail Desk or customer service can create for a delivered order.

## Used gear

We understand that you sometimes need to try gear outdoors to know it is right. Footwear and apparel that have been worn outdoors can be returned within 30 days for store credit, provided they are clean and undamaged. Tents can be returned within 30 days if they were pitched only once and are dry and clean. For safety reasons, stoves and climbing hardware that have been used can only be returned if they are defective, within 30 days.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued within 5 business days of the returned item arriving at our warehouse or being received in store. Card refunds can take a further 3 to 10 days to appear on your statement depending on your bank. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was faulty or we made a mistake with your order. Return postage is free for orders over 100 euros and otherwise costs a flat 6 euros, deducted from the refund.

## Exchanges

The fastest way to exchange an item for a different size or colour is to start a return and place a new order. Riverbend stores can also exchange items on the spot when the new item is in stock.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards, opened fuel canisters, personalised items and water filter cartridges that have been used cannot be returned. Sale items marked "final sale" cannot be returned unless they are faulty.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, or you received the wrong item, contact us within 14 days of delivery. We will send a replacement as soon as stock allows and collect the original item at no cost to you; you do not need to wait for the original to reach us before the replacement ships.

# Riverbend Outfitters Warranty Policy

Riverbend Outfitters stands behind every product it sells. Our warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship that appear during normal use for the warranty period of the product, counted from the date of purchase shown on your receipt or order confirmation. The warranty is provided to the original purchaser and is in addition to your statutory rights as a consumer.

## Warranty periods by product family

- Packs and bags, including daypacks, expedition packs and hydration vests: lifetime warranty for the life of the product.
- Tents and shelters: 2 years.
- Footwear, including hiking boots, trail runners and sandals: 1 year.
- Apparel, including rain shells, base layers and fleece: 2 years.
- Cooking equipment, including stoves, pots and water filters: 1 year.
- Climbing hardware and ropes: 1 year, and ropes must be retired according to the manufacturer's usage guidance regardless of warranty.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from accidents, improper use or care, alterations, or damage caused by animals. Examples of things we cannot cover are a worn durable water repellent (DWR) finish on a rain jacket (this is a maintenance item, see the care guide), mildew on a tent that was stored wet, soles worn smooth through use, and stoves that were modified or used with non-compatible fuel.

## How to make a claim

Start a claim online at riverbend.example.com/warranty or at any Riverbend store. You will need your order number or receipt, photos of the defect and a short description of how it occurred. Most claims are decided within 5 business days. If a claim is approved we will repair the product first; if a repair is not possible we will replace it with the same or an equivalent product, or issue store credit for the original purchase price. Shipping costs for approved warranty claims are paid by Riverbend. Please clean products before sending them in; we may return unwashed items without assessment.

## Products bought from other retailers

Riverbend-branded products bought from authorised retailers are covered by the same warranty periods. Keep the retailer's receipt as your proof of purchase, because we cannot look up orders placed with other shops.
